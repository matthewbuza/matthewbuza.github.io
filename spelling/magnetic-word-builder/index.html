Challenge Me and Fix It use the 1,800-word K–8 spelling bank supplied by the teacher, with definitions and parts of speech shown as provided. The lists are not labeled as official grade-level standards. For phonics instruction, some patterns (like ow, oo, and y) can represent more than one sound.
Word-bank content: supplied CSV. The CSV cites WordNet Release 3.0 / Princeton University licensing terms; retain the original CSV and its license notice when redistributing related material. This app works in 2D without external libraries; to enable 3D offline, provide an authorized local copy of Three.js at vendor/three.module.js.
