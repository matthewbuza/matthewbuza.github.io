What is happening? Shortwave sunlight passes through the clear cover and is absorbed by the dark interior. The interior warms and gives off infrared heat. The cover also reduces heat loss by limiting air movement and slowing the escape of thermal energy. More incoming sunlight means the oven can reach a higher equilibrium temperature.

Try it: Move the slider high, watch the temperature climb, then lower the sunlight and watch the oven cool.
