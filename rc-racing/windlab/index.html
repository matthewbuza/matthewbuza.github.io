06 / Teacher's note

Teaching visualization, not CFD. Mesh silhouette sampling, air trails, force curves and color values are heuristic visual models, not CFD. They illustrate frontal area, flow separation and wake effects, but do not compute actual aerodynamic pressure, drag coefficient or skin friction. Don't use the numbers for engineering predictions.
