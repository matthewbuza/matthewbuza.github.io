What this map really shows

The Sun sits at (0, 0, 0). Nearby stars are plotted using catalog positions and distances. The star spheres are intentionally much larger than scale so you can select them. Their spacing is otherwise distance-based.

The 3D visit mode is an illustration, not a scaled model of stellar radii, orbital separations, or real-time convection.
