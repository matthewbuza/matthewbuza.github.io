# Phase 6D — Period 4 complete

Completed 10 September 2026 in the existing Periodic Table Visualizer project. All **36 elements from hydrogen through krypton** now have explorers, with **129 isotope choices**. Rubidium through oganesson retain information cards. The app still launches directly from `index.html` using `file://`, with no installation, build step, server or runtime internet access.

## Science and teaching

All new elements distinguish the **filled inner 3d subshell** from outer **4s and 4p valence electrons**. The filled 3d subshell contains 10 electrons in five related orbitals. Bohr retains every physical electron; a filled-3d category selects an individual representative. Wave selects its complete occupied state. Quantum Explore Subshells shows one representative multi-lobed d shape with full subshell occupancy in text. The p view shows paired lobes; Overview keeps all seven zinc or eight gallium-through-krypton subshells.

Across Period 4 now explains K/Ca 4s filling, the 3d series through zinc, and Ga–Kr 4p filling at three reading levels. Chromium's 3d⁵ 4s¹ exception and copper's 3d¹⁰ 4s¹ configuration remain intact. Krypton closes the outer 4s² 4p⁶ configuration; this does not mean every possible n=4 subshell is occupied. Existing navigation and table order retain Ni → Cu → Zn → Ga. The existing app has no dedicated Previous/Next atom controls; none were added.

Engineering text covers zinc corrosion protection and batteries; gallium compound light emitters and electronics; germanium's transistor history, optics and detectors; arsenic doping and toxicity; selenium photoconductivity and materials; bromine compounds and environmental concerns; and krypton lighting, lasers and insulated windows. Text distinguishes pure elements from compounds and explains that material properties also depend on many atoms interacting. Elementary, Middle School and High School explanations remain separate data, with short particle descriptions.

## Source decisions

- **NIST Atomic Data Handbook:** all seven neutral ground-state configurations. NIST's older isotope-abundance entries are not used in place of CIAAW.
- **CIAAW / IUPAC:** terrestrial isotope fractions and uncertainty. Bromine uses the published ranges, not invented fixed percentages. Ge-76, Se-82 and Kr-78 remain in natural comparisons with explicit radioactive or reported-decay labels. Produced isotopes have no invented natural percentage; Kr-85 has only qualified environmental traces.
- **NNDC / ENSDF:** Zn-65 243.93 days; As-74 17.77 days; Se-75 119.78 days; ground-state Br-82 35.282 hours. As-74's electron-capture/positron and beta-minus branches are both retained.
- **BIPM / DDEP and EMA:** Ga-67 3.2613 days, Ga-68 67.71 minutes, Ge-68 270.95 days, Kr-85 10.752 years. Ga-68 uses the EMA value rather than the older DDEP value of 67.83 minutes. Source provenance remains in the local records.
- **GERDA:** Ge-76 observed two-neutrino double-beta half-life 2.022 × 10²¹ years. **CUPID-0:** Se-82 observed two-neutrino double-beta half-life 8.60 × 10¹⁹ years. Searches for neutrinoless decay are explicitly distinguished from observed decay.
- **Barabash's 2020 review, section 3.14:** Kr-78 double-K capture is presented as reported evidence needing independent confirmation, with a reported estimate of 1.9 × 10²² years. It is not presented as an unqualified established result. The selector, stability text, abundance labels, isotope descriptions and source notes preserve this qualification.
- **RSC, USGS, DOE/NIDC, NASA, IAEA, DailyMed, EMA and primary research:** element uses, isotope applications, NMR, medical compounds, generators, tracers, industrial measurements and planetary context. As-74 applications remain described as research rather than routine medicine.

Full titles, URLs, access dates and claim-specific notes are stored locally in `js/period4-completion-data.js`, displayed by the existing source panel, and catalogued in `SOURCES.txt`. Values displayed to students are rounded learning values, not precision nuclear-data recommendations.

## Verification and limits

Executed Chrome/Playwright checks using direct local files:

- All 36 enabled elements open through Explore and native mouse double-click. All 118 information cards remain; no 3D badges. Rb, U and invalid explorer routes are guarded.
- All 35 new isotopes across all three models: **105 model cases**, exact p/n/e counts, all Bohr electrons, all occupied wave states and all quantum layers. **70 proton/neutron interior round trips** preserve isotope and view state.
- **21 native canvas electron clicks**, **42 fullscreen interior round trips**, three explanation levels, subshell isolation, animation, keyboard/touch emulation, history/reload and state restoration. Six deliberately invalid isotope records are rejected.
- Independent configuration, default-isotope, filled-3d, outer-valence, natural-mixture/range and shape checks. Kr-78's evidence qualification is checked in all three reading levels.
- Responsive checks at 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1366 and 1920 pixels; expanded isotope and particle panels checked at 320/390/768. No document or particle-panel horizontal overflow. Native controls retain accessible labels and table targets of at least 44 pixels. Screenshot review includes Overview, isolated 3d/4p and narrow-screen reading views.
- No normal-use console errors, failed local resources or runtime network requests in the new-element functional checks.

All 14 earlier regression suites passed, from the original verification through Phase 6C (including its interaction and role suites). Copper's three saved canvas baselines still match byte-for-byte. The 360-element-switch performance run retained 1,718 listeners and three cached renderers, with 0.82 MiB post-GC heap growth. New-element median draw times were 0.5–0.7 ms Bohr, 0.6–0.7 ms Wave, and 4.5–5.0 ms Quantum. No progressive slowdown was detected by the before/after test threshold.

Detailed performance and regression outcomes are recorded in `TEST-RESULTS.txt` and the QA JSON files. The full-detail guarantee applies through krypton: no physical particle omission or grouped inner quantum cloud is used for these elements.

Physical phones/tablets, screen-reader operation, Firefox, Safari and Edge were not tested. Headless desktop Chrome timings do not establish performance on low-end classroom hardware. Clouds and standing waves remain qualitative teaching shapes, not solved many-electron wavefunctions; scale is compressed. No ions, molecules, lattice, bands, reactions, decay animations or new models were added.
