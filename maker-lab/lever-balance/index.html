Classroom challenge: In Play Mode, try to reach a 100% balance score. Move the fulcrum and predict which side will fall. Then select Force Equilibrium: the left weight stays fixed, and the right counterweight changes automatically as you move the fulcrum. Ask: Why can a smaller weight balance a larger one?

Model: a 6 m massless beam, with weights at both ends. Balance happens when left mass × left distance = right mass × right distance. The illustration exaggerates tilt for visibility; friction, beam weight, and momentum are ignored. In equilibrium mode the right counterweight may exceed the Play Mode slider range, so its value is displayed automatically.
