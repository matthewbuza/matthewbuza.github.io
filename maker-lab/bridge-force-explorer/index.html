Teaching model: Colors are a simplified, qualitative visualization of load effects, not a finite-element engineering analysis or a bridge safety rating. Red means a larger modeled effect within the current demonstration. The deck bar shows local bending/load influence; colored members and arrows illustrate typical force pathways. The left/right percentages are idealized vertical support reactions for a simply supported span, not a measure of how strong each support is. Real suspension and arch bridges also produce complex horizontal forces and depend on geometry, stiffness, connections, and foundations.
