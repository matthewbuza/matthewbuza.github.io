Orientation: the breadboard remains in its original orientation with the center trench running horizontally. The 555 has been rotated 90° clockwise so its long axis runs left-to-right and its two rows of four pins sit on opposite sides of the trench. The notch is at the left.

Inside the 555 Timer

Simplified functional diagram of the classic bipolar 555 architecture.
1/3 VCC: If TRIGGER falls below this level, the lower comparator sets the latch and the output goes HIGH.
2/3 VCC: If THRESHOLD rises above this level, the upper comparator resets the latch and the output goes LOW.
Why “555”? The classic internal divider uses three 5 kΩ resistors in series between VCC and ground.
