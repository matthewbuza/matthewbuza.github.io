2 · What is happening?

The potentiometer creates an analog voltage from 0 to 5 V. The ADC maps that voltage to one of 2N digital codes. More bits means smaller voltage steps.

This is an idealized ADC model. Real ADCs have conversion timing, electrical noise, and accuracy limits. Changing the bit depth here is educational; a classic Arduino Uno has a 10-bit ADC.
