Teaching notes: The ADC converts the voltage on A0 into a 10-bit reading (0–1023). map() scales that integer to an 8-bit PWM command (0–255). analogWrite() on pin 9 switches between LOW and HIGH; it does not produce a continuously variable analog voltage. The LED appears dimmer or brighter because the HIGH-time percentage changes. PWM waveform is drawn schematically, not at real-time 490 Hz. Connect an LED with an appropriate series resistor.
